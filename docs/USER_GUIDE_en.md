# Searching Taiwan Court Judgments with AI (User Guide)

Legal Detective's judgment retrieval service (TLR) lets you search and cite
22 million Taiwan court decisions **with your own AI** (ChatGPT / Claude / or
developer tools). No coding and no API key required.

> ⚠️ Important: this service only "finds and serves judgments." **Answers are
> generated by your own AI; Legal Detective does not endorse the AI's answer
> content.** For any legal conclusion, always open the judgment link and
> verify the full text yourself. This is not legal advice.

---

## Option A: Claude Desktop (recommended, no install)

1. Open the **Claude Desktop** app.
2. Top-left menu → **Customize** → **Connectors**.
3. Click **+** at the top right → **Add custom connector**.
4. Fill in:
   - **Name**: `Legal Detective` (any name you like)
   - **Remote MCP server URL**: `https://tlr.dr-lawbot.com/mcp`
5. Click **Add**. Done — no OAuth setup, no API key.

Then just ask in natural language (Chinese works best for Taiwan case law),
for example:

> Find judgments where the defendant was acquitted of Banking Act violations,
> and cite 3 docket numbers.

Claude will search the corpus, read the relevant judgments, answer with its
own understanding, and attach clickable judgment links.

---

## Option B: ChatGPT (build a custom GPT)

1. Create a **Custom GPT** in ChatGPT (requires ChatGPT Plus).
2. Edit → **Actions** → **Create new action** → **Import from URL**.
3. Paste: `https://tlr.dr-lawbot.com/openapi.yaml`
4. Set **Authentication** to **None**.
5. Save, then ask questions inside that GPT; it will call judgment search
   automatically.

---

## Option C: Command line (developers / advanced)

If you prefer a terminal, install the open-source CLI and pack judgments into
a file you can paste to any AI:

```bash
pip3 install twlegalrag

# search for judgments
twlegalrag search "銀行法 無罪" -n 5

# pack into a bundle (for your own AI)
twlegalrag pack "違反銀行法被判無罪的案件" -o bundle.json
```

Paste the whole `bundle.json` to ChatGPT / Claude and require it to "cite
only judgments inside the bundle."

## FAQ

**Q: Can I look up a specific docket number directly?**
A: Yes (v1.1). Enter the complete docket number, e.g.
最高法院112年度台上字第9號, and the exact documents for that docket are
retrieved (civil/criminal cases sharing a number are listed side by side with
labels). **If the reply says the docket was not found, it means the database
has no record — not that the judgment does not exist. Do not let the AI
describe the case from memory.**

**Q: How do I know whether a judgment was vacated on appeal or is final?**
A: When reading a judgment's full text, a `case_history` (appeal chain) is
attached: it lists the upper/lower decisions recorded in the database, and a
judgment vacated on appeal is flagged as 主文含「廢棄」 — its holding has been
overturned and the AI should not cite it as currently valid. Note: **no
upper-court record only means the database has no record; it does not mean
the judgment is final.**

**Q: The AI cited a judgment — how do I confirm it did not misread the
court's holding?**
A: Since v1.1 every bundle carries built-in "opinion-layer self-check"
instructions requiring the AI, after answering, to verify that each holding
really comes from the cited judgment, that win/lose/vacated directions are
not reversed, and that vacated judgments are not cited as valid. This is
still **best-effort** — always open the judgment links yourself and check the
main text and reasoning, especially for conclusions you intend to rely on.

**Q: I asked for "acquittals under the Banking Act" but got convictions /
civil cases?**
A: Search finds what is semantically related; it is not a precise result
filter. The AI reads the full texts and filters afterwards, but **always open
the judgment links and verify** — especially yes/no conclusions like "was it
an acquittal": read the judgment's main text; do not rely on the AI's summary
alone.

**Q: Are my questions logged?**
A: Your search strings are sent to the retrieval service and may be logged to
improve retrieval quality (no personal identity attached; not used to train
generative models). **Do not include personal secrets or confidential facts
in queries.**

**Q: Does it cost money?**
A: Judgment retrieval is free and requires no registration. AI usage costs
are borne by your own ChatGPT / Claude account — this service charges no AI
fees and does not call any AI for you.

**Q: A judgment link does not open / I want the full text?**
A: Every result carries a `dr-legal.com.tw` judgment link; open it to read the
complete full text.
